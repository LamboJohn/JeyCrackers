Firework Safety Guide

Your safety is our priority. Follow these essential tips for a joyful celebration.

🛡️ Handling & Lighting Tips

  • Always light fireworks outdoors in an open area away from buildings.
  • Keep a bucket of water or a garden hose nearby for emergencies.
  • Never try to re-light or pick up fireworks that have not fully ignited.
  • Wear cotton clothes while lighting crackers; avoid synthetic fabrics.
  • Maintain a safe distance of at least 5 meters after lighting.

⚠️ Critical Warning

Never light fireworks while holding them in your hand. Always place them on a flat surface. Children must always be supervised by an adult.

📦 Safe Storage Advice

  • Store fireworks in a cool, dry place away from any sources of heat.
  • Keep crackers in their original packaging until you are ready to use them.
  • Ensure they are stored in a location that is inaccessible to children.
  • Do not store fireworks near inflammable materials like oil or gas.
